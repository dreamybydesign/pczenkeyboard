Privacy Policy for PCZENKeyboard

Effective date: 2026-09-26

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how PCZENKeyboard (the "App") handles information. The App is developed and published by David Tesarik, an individual developer (referred to below as "I", "me" or "my").

In short: the App does not collect, store on any server, transmit, sell or share any personal data or any other data about you. Everything the App does happens on your device.

In this Privacy Policy, "collect" means obtaining data from your device in a way that makes it available to me or to anyone else, for example by transmitting it over the internet.

2. Who is responsible for the App

David Tesarik is the developer and publisher of the App and, to the extent that any data protection law applies, the person responsible for the App's data practices (the "controller" within the meaning of the EU General Data Protection Regulation, "GDPR"). You can contact me at: https://github.com/dreamybydesign/PCZENKeyboard/issues

3. Data the App does not collect

The App does not collect any data, and I do not receive any data from it. In particular, the App does not collect:

  • contact information, such as your name, email address, phone number or postal address;
  • identifiers, such as device identifiers, the advertising identifier, user IDs or IP addresses;
  • location data;
  • contacts, photos, videos, audio, messages, files or other user content;
  • health, fitness, financial or payment information;
  • browsing or search history;
  • usage data, analytics or diagnostics, such as crash reports or performance data;
  • any other information about you or your device.

The App has no user accounts, registration or sign-in, and it does not use cookies or similar technologies.

4. No internet connection and no third parties

The App does not connect to the internet. It does not send any data from your device to me or to anyone else, and it does not receive data from any server. The App contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, crash-reporting or social-media components, and no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data.

I do not sell, rent, share, license or otherwise disclose personal information to anyone, and I have never done so. The App does not "track" you as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, and it performs no targeted advertising and no profiling.

5. Information stored on your device

Any settings you choose or content you create while using the App are stored only on your device, in the App's own private storage area. I have no access to this information, and it is not transmitted anywhere. You can remove it at any time by deleting the App from your device, which also deletes the App's storage area.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, your operating system may include the App's local data in that backup in the same way as for any other app. Such backups are created by Apple's iCloud service or by your own computer, are under your control, are not accessible to me, and are not covered by this Privacy Policy.

6. Device permissions

If the App asks for your permission to use a feature of your device (for example the camera, microphone, photo library or location), it does so only to provide the function you requested. Anything obtained through such a permission is processed only on your device and is never transmitted. You can review, grant or withdraw permissions at any time in the Settings app on your device.

7. Information that Apple may collect

Apple Inc. operates the App Store and the iOS operating system. When you download, install, update or use apps, Apple may collect certain information independently of me, under Apple's own privacy policy and according to your device settings. This may include App Store statistics and, if you have opted in to sharing analytics with app developers, crash reports and app usage statistics. Apple may make such information available to me through its developer tools only in aggregated or anonymised form that does not identify you, and I do not attempt to identify you from it. You can review and change these settings on your device under Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements. Apple's privacy policy is available at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

8. Children's privacy

Because the App does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children either. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age, or under the higher age of consent that applies in some countries (up to 16 in parts of the European Union). If you believe that a child has provided me with personal information in some other way, please contact me at https://github.com/dreamybydesign/PCZENKeyboard/issues so that I can delete it.

9. Data retention and deletion

I retain no data about you, because I never receive any. Information stored locally by the App stays on your device until you delete it within the App (where the App offers such a function) or delete the App itself. The App never asks for your consent to collect data, so there is no consent to revoke. If you nevertheless want to make a request or ask a question, contact me at https://github.com/dreamybydesign/PCZENKeyboard/issues.

10. Your rights under the GDPR and similar laws

If you are in the European Economic Area, or in another country whose laws grant similar rights, you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to its processing, to receive a copy of it in a portable format, and to withdraw any consent you have given. Because I do not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for me to provide, correct or delete. If you nevertheless believe that I hold personal data about you, contact me at https://github.com/dreamybydesign/PCZENKeyboard/issues and I will respond without undue delay.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, your place of work or the place of the alleged infringement.

The App does not process personal data, so no legal basis for processing under Article 6 of the GDPR is required, and no international transfer of personal data takes place.

11. Notice for residents of the United States

If you live in California or in another U.S. state with a comprehensive privacy law: I do not collect, sell or share personal information, I do not use personal information for targeted advertising or profiling, and I do not collect sensitive personal information. I have never sold or shared personal information. Because no personal information is collected, there is no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" choice for you to make, and there is nothing to request under rights to know, correct or delete. The App performs no tracking of any kind, so "Do Not Track" and Global Privacy Control signals do not affect it.

12. Security

Your information never leaves your device through the App. Its protection therefore depends on the security of your device itself, such as your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and the data protection built into iOS.
